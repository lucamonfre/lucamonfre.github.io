jpeg of the head of an elf
2024
acrylic on canvas
-
an image of an elf distorted beyond recognition and compressed to the point of obscurity. this piece is intended to reflect a loss of ones sparkle - or a loss of an idealistic image of the world. staple imagery of the fantastic pushed beyond its ability to convey its form clearly. an image shared between so many its lost its ability to hold itself together. an image that is tearing itself apart each time its seen.
